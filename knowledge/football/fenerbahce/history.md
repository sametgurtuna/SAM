# Fenerbahçe SK — History

## Foundation and Early Years
Fenerbahçe Spor Kulübü was founded on May 3, 1907, in the Kadıköy district on the Asian side of Istanbul, Turkey. It is one of the oldest and most storied football clubs in Turkish history. The club was named after the Fenerbahçe neighborhood, which in turn derives from a lighthouse ("fener") in a garden ("bahçe"). The club colors are yellow and navy blue, earning the nickname "Sarı Kanaryalar" (Yellow Canaries).

## Founding Members
The club was founded by Nurizade Ziya Songülen, Enver Yetiş, and Ayetullah Bey, among others. In its earliest years, Fenerbahçe played informal matches against local teams and foreign clubs based in Istanbul, including British and Greek teams. Football was still a new sport in the Ottoman Empire, and Fenerbahçe became one of the pioneering Turkish clubs to adopt it.

## Growth into a National Institution
Throughout the 1920s and 1930s, Fenerbahçe established itself as a dominant force in Istanbul football leagues. The club became a symbol of Turkish national identity, especially during the era when Turkish clubs competed against non-Turkish Istanbul teams. Fenerbahçe's victories were seen as sources of national pride.

## The Professional Era
The Turkish Süper Lig was established in 1959, and Fenerbahçe was among the founding clubs. The club won the inaugural league championship and went on to become one of the "Big Three" of Turkish football alongside Galatasaray and Beşiktaş.

## Kadıköy — The Fortress
Fenerbahçe's home ground in Kadıköy is legendary. The club has historically been nearly unbeatable at home, with the intense atmosphere at their stadium making it one of the most intimidating venues in European football. The phrase "Kadıköy'de yenilmezlik" (invincibility at Kadıköy) is a point of enormous pride among supporters.

## Philosophy and Identity
Fenerbahçe is more than a football club — it is a multi-sport institution with successful teams in basketball, volleyball, swimming, and athletics. However, football remains the heart of the club's identity. The Fenerbahçe supporter culture is one of the most passionate in world football, with the club commanding one of the largest and most dedicated fan bases in Turkey.
