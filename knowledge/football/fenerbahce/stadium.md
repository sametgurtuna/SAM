# Fenerbahçe SK — Stadium

## Şükrü Saracoğlu Stadium (Ülker Stadyumu)
Fenerbahçe's home ground is the Şükrü Saracoğlu Stadium, located in the Kadıköy district on the Asian side of Istanbul. It is also known as Ülker Stadyumu due to a naming rights sponsorship.

## History
The stadium was originally built in 1908, making it one of the oldest football grounds in Turkey. It has undergone several major renovations. The most significant reconstruction took place between 1999 and 2006, transforming it into a modern UEFA 4-star stadium.

## Capacity and Design
The stadium holds approximately 50,530 spectators. It is an all-seater stadium with a distinct bowl design that amplifies crowd noise, making it one of the loudest and most intimidating venues in European football.

## The Kadıköy Atmosphere
The atmosphere at Şükrü Saracoğlu is legendary. Visiting teams frequently struggle with the intense noise and hostility of the home crowd. The ultras groups, particularly "Genç Fenerbahçeliler" and other organized supporter groups, create a wall of sound that has been praised and feared by players and coaches from across Europe. The chant "Kadıköy'e hoş geldiniz" (Welcome to Kadıköy) has become an intimidation phrase in Turkish football.

## UEFA Recognition
The stadium hosted the 2009 UEFA Cup Final (between Shakhtar Donetsk and Werder Bremen), a testament to its quality and capacity. It has also hosted numerous Turkish national team matches.

## The Lefter Küçükandonyadis Tribune
One of the main stands is named after Lefter Küçükandonyadis, Fenerbahçe's legendary player, honoring his immense contribution to the club's history.
