# Fenerbahçe SK — Modern Era (2010–2025)

## The 2010-11 Championship
Fenerbahçe won the 2010-11 Süper Lig title under Aykut Kocaman. This was the club's 19th league championship and remains their most recent title. The season was marked by strong team performances and fan enthusiasm.

## The 2011-12 Match-Fixing Case
In 2011, Fenerbahçe was caught up in a match-fixing investigation that shook Turkish football. Club president Aziz Yıldırım was arrested and convicted. Fenerbahçe was banned from European competition for two seasons by UEFA. The club has consistently maintained that the process was unjust and politically motivated. This episode remains one of the most painful and controversial chapters in the club's modern history, and Fenerbahçe fans continue to feel a sense of injustice about the sanctions.

## Aziz Yıldırım Era (1998–2018)
Aziz Yıldırım served as Fenerbahçe's president for 20 years, one of the longest tenures in Turkish football. Under his leadership, the club won multiple league titles, renovated the stadium, and competed regularly in European competition. His tenure was also marked by the match-fixing crisis.

## Ali Koç Era (2018–present)
Ali Koç, a businessman from the prominent Koç family, became Fenerbahçe's president in 2018. His tenure has been characterized by ambitious goals, significant investment, high-profile managerial appointments, and a push to modernize the club's governance and infrastructure.

## Notable Managers (2010–2025)
- **Aykut Kocaman** (2010-2013, 2017-2018): Won the 2010-11 championship. A Fenerbahçe legend as both player and manager.
- **Ersun Yanal** (2013-2014, 2019): Won the 2013-14 championship. Known for attacking football.
- **Vítor Pereira** (2015-2016): Portuguese manager who came close to the title.
- **Jorge Jesus** (2022-2023): Portuguese manager who brought an exciting playing style and came agonizingly close to the championship.
- **İsmail Kartal** (multiple stints): A steady hand who stabilized the team during turbulent periods.
- **José Mourinho** (2024-2025): The legendary Portuguese manager was appointed as Fenerbahçe head coach, one of the highest-profile appointments in Turkish football history.

## The Championship Drought
Since the 2013-14 season (won under Ersun Yanal), Fenerbahçe has not won the Süper Lig. This drought has been a source of frustration for the fan base, especially as rivals Galatasaray have won multiple titles during this period. Several seasons have seen Fenerbahçe come agonizingly close — finishing as runners-up multiple times.

## European Campaigns
Fenerbahçe has continued to participate in European competition, primarily in the Europa League and Conference League. The club has had varying degrees of success, with occasional strong group stage performances but has struggled to recapture the heights of the 2007-08 Champions League quarter-final run.
