# Fenerbahçe SK — Rivalries

## Fenerbahçe vs Galatasaray — The Intercontinental Derby
The rivalry between Fenerbahçe and Galatasaray is known as the "Kıtalar Arası Derbi" (Intercontinental Derby) because Fenerbahçe is based on the Asian side of Istanbul and Galatasaray on the European side. It is the biggest and most intense derby in Turkish football and one of the most passionate rivalries in world football.

The derby has produced countless dramatic moments, controversial incidents, and emotional highs and lows for fans of both clubs. Matches between the two clubs regularly attract massive television audiences and generate intense media coverage. For Fenerbahçe fans, victories against Galatasaray are among the most celebrated moments in any season.

The rivalry extends beyond football into basketball, volleyball, and other sports where both clubs compete.

## Fenerbahçe vs Beşiktaş — The Istanbul Derby
The rivalry with Beşiktaş is another significant Istanbul derby. While perhaps not as intense as the Galatasaray rivalry, matches against Beşiktaş are always fiercely contested. Both clubs share a mutual respect but compete intensely on the pitch.

## The Big Three (Üç Büyükler)
Fenerbahçe, Galatasaray, and Beşiktaş are collectively known as the "Üç Büyükler" (Big Three) of Turkish football. These three clubs have historically dominated the Süper Lig and represent the pinnacle of Turkish club football. They account for the vast majority of league titles and have the largest fan bases in the country.

## European Rivalries
Through Champions League and Europa League campaigns, Fenerbahçe has developed notable competitive histories with clubs like Chelsea, Sevilla, Arsenal, Manchester United, and various other European sides. European nights at Kadıköy are particularly memorable for the hostile atmosphere visiting teams face.
