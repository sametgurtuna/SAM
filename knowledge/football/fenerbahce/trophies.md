# Fenerbahçe SK — Trophies and Titles

## Süper Lig Championships
Fenerbahçe has won the Turkish Süper Lig championship 19 times, making them one of the most successful clubs in the history of Turkish football. Championship seasons: 1959, 1960-61, 1963-64, 1964-65, 1967-68, 1969-70, 1973-74, 1974-75, 1977-78, 1982-83, 1984-85, 1988-89, 1995-96, 2000-01, 2003-04, 2004-05, 2006-07, 2010-11, 2013-14.

The club also holds the record for the longest unbeaten home run in Turkish football.

## Turkish Cup (Türkiye Kupası)
Fenerbahçe has won the Turkish Cup 7 times: 1968, 1974, 1979, 1983, 2012, 2013, 2023.

## Turkish Super Cup
Fenerbahçe has won the Turkish Super Cup 9 times.

## European Competition
Fenerbahçe has participated in numerous UEFA Champions League and Europa League campaigns. Key European moments include:
- Reaching the Champions League quarter-finals in the 2007-08 season, one of the finest achievements in Turkish club football history. They defeated Sevilla in the round of 16 before being eliminated by Chelsea.
- Multiple group stage appearances in the Champions League.
- Various Europa League campaigns with notable knockout round runs.

## Notable Records
- Fenerbahçe holds the record for the most consecutive home wins in the Turkish Süper Lig.
- The club has one of the highest average attendances in Turkish football.
- Fenerbahçe's youth academy has produced numerous Turkish internationals.
