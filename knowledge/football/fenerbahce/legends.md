# Fenerbahçe SK — Legends

## Alex de Souza
Alex Roberto de Souza, commonly known as Alex, is widely regarded as Fenerbahçe's greatest ever player and one of the finest foreign footballers in Turkish football history. The Brazilian attacking midfielder joined Fenerbahçe in 2004 and stayed until 2012, becoming the club's all-time top scorer with 171 goals in all competitions. Alex wore the number 10 shirt and captained the team for several seasons. He won three Süper Lig titles with Fenerbahçe (2004-05, 2006-07, 2010-11). His free kicks, vision, leadership, and love for the club made him an icon. Fenerbahçe fans consider him one of their own, not a foreigner.

## Lefter Küçükandonyadis
Lefter Küçükandonyadis (1925–2012) is one of the most iconic figures in Turkish football history and a Fenerbahçe legend. A Greek-Turkish striker, Lefter played for Fenerbahçe from 1947 to 1964, scoring over 400 goals in unofficial tallies. He was known for his extraordinary dribbling, goal-scoring ability, and sportsmanship. The Fenerbahçe Şükrü Saracoğlu Stadium's stand was named "Lefter Küçükandonyadis Tribune" in his honor. He is considered one of the greatest players ever to wear the yellow and navy blue.

## Can Bartu
Can Bartu (1936–2019) was a legendary Turkish sportsman who excelled in both football and basketball. As a footballer, he played for Fenerbahçe and later had a successful stint with Fiorentina in Serie A. Bartu was known for his elegant playing style, technical skill, and intelligence on the pitch. He is one of the few athletes in the world to have represented their country at international level in two different sports.

## Rıdvan Dilmen
Rıdvan Dilmen is one of Fenerbahçe's most celebrated players from the 1980s and early 1990s. A gifted playmaker and goal scorer, Dilmen was the face of Fenerbahçe football during his era. He is known for his technical brilliance, particularly his ball control and finishing. After retirement, he became one of Turkey's most prominent football commentators. He remains deeply associated with Fenerbahçe's identity.

## Other Notable Legends
- **Tuncay Şanlı**: Prolific striker who came through Fenerbahçe's youth academy.
- **Roberto Carlos**: The legendary Brazilian left-back played for Fenerbahçe from 2007 to 2009, adding star power and winning the league.
- **Zico**: The great Brazilian also managed Fenerbahçe in the 2006-07 season.
- **Semih Şentürk**: Known as "Semih the Scorpion," a fan favorite striker in the 2000s.
- **Emre Belözoğlu**: A combative midfielder who had two significant stints at the club, known for leadership and passion.
